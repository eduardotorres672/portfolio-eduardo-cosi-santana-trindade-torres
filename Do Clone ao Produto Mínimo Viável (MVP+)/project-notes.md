# AI Ethics Project

An interactive application built with Google AI Studio exploring artificial intelligence, authorship, and ethical boundaries in the use of AI tools.

## Live Demo

[View the project on Google AI Studio](https://ai.studio/apps/b44818c6-d548-4993-b636-1955fe818478)

## About

This project addresses key ethical principles around AI-assisted development and content creation:

- **Authorship Registration** — Documenting inventions before publishing to ensure proper credit for original creators.
- **Human Touch** — Reserving core ideas and final design decisions for humans, valuing originality over generic output.
- **Task Division** — Using AI for repetitive tasks while leaving real creative innovation to human talent.
- **Fair Profit** — Advocating for systems where companies profiting from AI compensate the authors whose work served as training data.

> Reverse engineering assisted by AI stops being learning and becomes plagiarism the moment the goal shifts from understanding to copying. Analyzing a product to learn the technique behind it is study. Using AI to recreate and distribute or monetize that product is plagiarism.
